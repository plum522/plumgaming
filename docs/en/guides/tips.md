# Tips & Tricks

___
## How to Level Up the Fastest

All jobs earn 800 XP per run. Therefore, the fastest way to level up is to repeatedly perform the job with the shortest distance.

___
## How to Stop the Rain

When it’s raining, there are many inconveniences, such as slippery roads, poor visibility, and a dirty truck.
If you return to the main menu and restart the game, the weather will clear up.

___
## How to Add a Car Wash to Your Garage

When you max out your garage level, the garage will expand, and a car wash will be added to the premises.

___
## How to Remove Traffic Jams

Traffic jams are common in this game. Open Photo Mode by tapping the “⋯” button in the top-right corner, then turn off “traffic” or “congestion” to make all NPC vehicles disappear.
> Since the parking brake is engaged during Photo Mode, using this feature while driving at high speeds can be dangerous.

___
## How to Move NPC Vehicles Out of the Way

When you want to pass on a narrow road but can’t, honking the horn or flashing your high beams (using the button in the bottom-left corner of the screen) will cause the NPC vehicle in front of you to move slightly to the side.

___
## When You Can’t Climb a Hill

When transporting heavy cargo, your truck may sometimes be unable to make it to the top of a hill.
Tap “Detach Trailer” from the “⋯” button in the top-right corner. Once detached, drive all the way to the top of the hill, then use “Towing Service” from the same “⋯” button.
This will allow you to return to the road with the trailer reattached.

___
## In-Game Time

In this game, time passes 12 times faster than in the real world.
Additionally, sunrise is at 5:00 a.m. and sunset is at 8:00 p.m.
