# Efficient Ways to Earn Money and Progress

It’s difficult to earn money in Truckers of Europe 3, <br>
especially in the early stages when you can only take low-paying jobs, so <br>
・How to reach Level 12—when restrictions are virtually lifted—as quickly as possible<br>
・How to secure the highest-paying jobs in the game after Level 12<br>
.

___
## Day 1 to Level 12

### If You’re Short on Cash Right After Starting
If your funds are €4,000 or less, earn at least €4,000 through jobs<br>

### Once you have €4,000
Go to the trailer dealer and purchase the cheapest trailer, the “Curtain Side,” for €4,000 <br>

### After purchasing the trailer
Using the “Curtain Side,” prioritize **short-distance jobs** and repeat this until you reach Level 12. <br>


>#### Key Points
> - Since the experience points earned from jobs are the same for all jobs, the fastest way to level up is to keep the distances short.<br>
> - Even for the same job, your earnings are roughly doubled if you use your own trailer, so be sure to purchase the cheapest trailer available.

___
## Level 12 and Up

Once you reach Level 12 or higher, go to the trailer dealer and purchase an “Oversize” trailer<br>
and set it to “5×3” via customization, then accept high-paying jobs for “Oversize.”

- This trailer has the highest payload capacity of all trailers.
- Since higher payload capacity increases job rewards, this trailer earns the most in the game.
>⚠️Note: Jobs for this trailer are quite demanding, so you’ll also need to upgrade your truck via customization. <br>
>⚠️Once the “Oversize” is upgraded to 5x3, you can only accept jobs in the three cities of Zurich, Munich, and Stuttgart.<br>
>With configurations other than 5x3, you can accept jobs in other cities as well.

___
## Summary

### Day 1 – Level 12
Own the cheapest trailer and repeatedly take only short-distance jobs to earn experience and money.

### Level 12 and Up
Upgrade to an Oversize (5x3) trailer and earn big with high-weight jobs.
