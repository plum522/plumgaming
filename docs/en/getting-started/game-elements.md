# In-Game Elements

This section introduces the basic system elements found in this game.

___
## Currency

<img src="/assets/images/getting-started/your-balance.JPG" width="450"><br>
The game uses € (euros).<br>
You can earn them as rewards from jobs or purchase them with real money.<br>
If you attempt to make a payment when your balance is 0, your balance will go into the negative.<br>
(If your balance is insufficient when purchasing a new vehicle or garage, the purchase will be denied.)

___
## Fuel (Gasoline)

An essential element for driving. <br>
We recommend refueling at a gas station every 500 km. <br>
You can refuel for free by watching ads.

___
## Gas Stations

<img src="/assets/images/getting-started/gasolina-station.jpeg" width="450"><br>
You can refuel with gasoline, which is essential in the game. <br>
They are located in some cities and along highways. <br>

___
## Sleep

<img src="/assets/images/getting-started/bed.jpeg" width="250"><br>
In this game, you need to sleep for up to 6 hours every day in-game time.
If you drive for long periods without sleeping, drowsiness will cause your vision to go completely dark.
Since this can lead to accidents, try to sleep before the sleep gauge at the bottom of the screen reaches 0.
You can sleep at gas stations, garages, or locations marked with a bed icon along the highway.

___
## Car Wash

In this game, your truck gets dirty as you drive. <br>
You can wash your truck at a car wash. <br>
<img src="/assets/images/getting-started/washer.jpeg" width="450"><br>

___
## Weather

<img src="/assets/images/getting-started/weather-EN.png" width="450"><br>
Weather conditions include sunny, cloudy, rainy, and thunderstorms. <br>
The default is sunny. <br>
You can also stop the rain. <br>
[How to Stop the Rain](/en/guides/tips/#How-to-Stop-the-Rain)

___
## Highway Toll Gates

<img src="/assets/images/getting-started/toll-gate.jpeg" width="450"><br>
You’ll pass through one at least once when driving on the highway. <br>
Tolls range from €70 to €140, depending on the vehicle’s weight.

___
## Ferries

<img src="/assets/images/getting-started/ferry.png" width="450"><br>
Found in certain parts of the map. <br>
The fare is €500, but you can ride for free or skip the fare by watching an ad. <br>
The location is around the red circle in the image. <br>
The one-way trip takes 3.5 minutes (40 minutes in-game time). <br>
<img src="/assets/images/getting-started/ferry-location.png" width="450">
