## Job Basics

Job rewards are generally proportional to
- Distance
- Cargo weight

Additionally, jobs have restrictions based on your player level. <br>
There are six levels: 2, 3, 5, 7, 12, and 17. <br>
<br>
The higher your level, the more challenging the jobs you can accept. <br>
Once you exceed Level 5, you’ll be able to transport moderately heavy cargo, such as construction materials. <br>
Starting at Level 12, you’ll be able to transport heavy cargo, such as heavy machinery. <br>
Starting at Level 17, you’ll be able to transport the “Wagon” (a railroad locomotive), which has the maximum weight capacity. <br>
