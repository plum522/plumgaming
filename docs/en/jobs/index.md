# What are “Jobs”?

Jobs are an essential part of progressing through this game.  
You’ll need the rewards earned from jobs to upgrade your trucks, purchase new trucks and trailers, upgrade or buy new bases, and cover gas costs.  
In this game, a “job” refers to transporting cargo from one city to another, just like in real-life trucking.  
Of course, jobs aren’t just a way to earn money in the game—doing them is part of the fun.  
Additionally, as you level up, you’ll be able to take on more challenging jobs and those that offer higher rewards.

## Job Specifications

Rewards for jobs vary significantly depending on the type and distance of each job.
On this site, we independently analyzed approximately 3,000 jobs to investigate the characteristics of jobs in this game and every job that exists.

> This analysis was conducted based on version 0.70.64, but specifications may change in future updates.

Additionally, some jobs may have specific requirements that must be met in order to accept them.
Currently, the only requirement to accept a job is the player’s “level.”
While there are many jobs available starting at Level 1, some jobs have restrictions based on player level,
with 10 tiers: Levels 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 10, 12, 15, 17, and 30.
If your level is below the required tier, you cannot accept that job.
> You can remove these restrictions by making in-game purchases.

Furthermore, the higher a job’s required level, the more difficult the job becomes.
A job’s required level affects not only its difficulty but also its rewards; for jobs requiring the maximum level of “Level 30,” the reward multiplier is two to three times higher than for other jobs covering the same distance.
> For jobs other than those requiring Level 30, the reward multiplier may not change based on the required level.
> This is because there are other factors besides the job’s required level that determine the reward multiplier.
