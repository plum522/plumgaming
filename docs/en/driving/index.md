## Driving Basics

In this game, we recommend driving at 80 km/h on highways and 50–60 km/h on local roads.
Also, using cruise control to maintain a constant speed improves fuel efficiency. <br>
*On rainy days, tires are more prone to slipping, so we recommend reducing your speed.

___
## Passing Through Toll Booths

The barriers at highway toll booths raise automatically, but the timing is quite tight, so
you’ll hit the barrier unless you slow down to 20 km/h at the toll booth.

>Some toll booths have lanes without barriers, so if you use those, you won’t need to slow down.

___
## When It Rains

・Visibility worsens, so use your windshield wipers according to the intensity of the rain.
・Both trucks and trailers get dirty more easily.
・Tires are more likely to slip.
If you really can’t stand it, change the weather.
[How to Stop the Rain](/en/guides/tips/#How_to_Stop_the_Rain)

___
## Sleep

In this game, you need to sleep for up to 6 hours every day based on in-game time.
If you drive for long periods without sleeping, drowsiness will cause your vision to go completely dark.
Since this can lead to accidents, try to sleep before the sleep gauge at the bottom of the screen reaches 0.
You can sleep at gas stations, garages, and on the side of the highway
at locations marked with a bed icon.

___
## Damage & Damage Penalties

Your truck or trailer will take damage if you crash into something. <br>
If you sustain damage while transporting cargo, you’ll receive a damage penalty that slightly reduces your job pay. <br>
You can reset the damage penalty to 0 by watching an ad.

___
## Time Penalty

Similar to the damage penalty, if your delivery is delayed, you’ll receive a penalty based on the length of the delay. <br>
You won’t incur a delay if you drive normally. <br>
Like the damage penalty, you can reset this to 0 by watching an ad.
